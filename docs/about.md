This portal helps Schools keep an eye on every module's Blackboard course: how
accessible it is, whether it has a reading list, and whether it follows the
University's Blackboard template.

### What this is

Rather than treating a VLE audit as a single once-a-year form to fill in, the
portal pulls together data the University already holds, so problems can be
spotted and supported early rather than recorded once at the start of the year.

It's a monitoring and support tool, not a scoring exercise aimed at
individuals. A finding on a module is a prompt to help, not a verdict on
whoever teaches it.

### Who it's for

* **Digital Learning Advisors (DLAs)** work through the School Dashboard, and
  spot-check a sample of modules by hand each semester.
* **School Leadership** use the School Dashboard to see how their whole school
  is doing, and where a module needs attention, without having to check each
  module by hand.
* **Faculty-wide leadership** use the Faculty Overview to compare across all
  schools at once.
* **Module Leads** can look up their own module on the **Module report** page
  to see its current accessibility, reading list and template status, and
  what's outstanding.

**A note for module leads:** if a module you lead comes up in a review, you
don't need to have filled anything in yourself. Audits are carried out by a
Digital Learning Advisor on your behalf, using what the portal's data shows
plus their own check of the module. Any finding raised with you comes from
that combination, not a personal judgement about your teaching.

### Where the information comes from

Four systems the University already runs: **SITS** for the module list itself,
**Ally** for how accessible course materials are to students using assistive
technology, **Leganto** for whether a module has a reading list and whether
it's published, and the faculty's **Template Alignment Report** for whether
sections of the Blackboard template are filled in and visible to students.

Each is refreshed periodically rather than read live, so everything here
describes the module as at the latest snapshot.

### Why a data-driven approach

The faculty has hundreds of modules and a small team of Digital Learning
Advisors, so checking every module by hand, every year, in the same depth
simply isn't possible.

Data like Ally and the Template Alignment Report are good at telling you
**whether something is there and whether it's been touched**. What they can't
tell you is whether the content is actually good: accurate, current, well
explained. That judgement still needs a person. So the data does the bulk of
the routine checking at scale, which frees advisors up for the modules that
genuinely need a closer look.

### Where to go next

Use the sidebar to get started. If you're a module lead, go to **Module
report** and choose your module.

Under **Resources & Support** you'll find the **FAQs**, which have a section
for each role, and **Help & Support**, a fuller walkthrough of every page and
where each figure comes from.
